How the model works

This is a teaching model, not a replacement for Moldflow or Moldex3D. It follows the flow path along the mesh from the gate, splits the part into slices by distance, and treats each slice as a slit with its own wall thickness taken from the STL. Melt is Nylon 66 with a shear-thinning, temperature-dependent viscosity. A frozen skin grows from the wall after the front passes, so thin slices close first.

  • Injection stage: fixed flow rate, injection-stage volume ÷ injection time, until the pressure limit is reached.
  • Holding stage: constant holding pressure for the holding time. It can only push melt through the channel that is still open.
  • Thin wall value = longest flow path ÷ nominal wall (2 × volume ÷ surface area). For the tie wrap this lands near the 385 of the training case.
  • The skin growth that restricts flow is calibrated so the training case shows its expected behaviour, so treat pressures as relative, not absolute. The colours show melt temperature estimated from conduction through each wall, so thin sections cool first and the thick head stays hot. Venting is assumed ideal and shrinkage is ignored.
  • STL units are assumed to be inches unless you choose millimetres.
